Transport and distribution you can plan around

Our fleet

Our trucks, on Uganda’s roads

Most of the trucks we operate are our own, owned and maintained by the company: box trucks and flatbeds for dry cargo, and fuel tankers for petroleum products. When a customer’s work needs more capacity than our own fleet can cover, we lease or rent additional trucks for the short term to meet that demand. Trucks in our own fleet carry GPS units, so we and our customers can follow each delivery in real time.

How we work

Built on safety, customer focus and long-term relationships

Devoted to customer success

Every customer has its own delivery pattern and its own constraints. We start by understanding those requirements, then plan transport that keeps their supply chain moving and reduces operational risk.

Engagement is key

We work closely with customers to review performance, raise issues early and agree practical, cost-effective solutions. Real-time tracking means customers can see their deliveries as they happen.

Professional service at competitive rates

Trained drivers, maintained and GPS-tracked trucks, and clear documentation, priced to support our customers’ distribution costs.
