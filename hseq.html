Our policies

How we manage risk

Emergency response

Emergency response plans protect life, limit environmental impact and safeguard assets. Drills, training and preparedness reviews keep teams ready.

Accident and incident reporting

All accidents, incidents and near misses are reported, investigated and reviewed to prevent them happening again.

Fatigue management

Fatigue is a major transport risk. Scheduling rules, rest requirements and supervision support driver wellbeing and safe operations.

Drug and alcohol

We apply zero tolerance to alcohol and substance misuse to protect our employees, customers and the public.

Hazard and risk management

Hazards are identified, assessed, controlled and reviewed through inspections, observations, incident investigations and employee engagement.

Driver training

Continuous training gives drivers the competence, knowledge and professionalism to work safely and effectively.
