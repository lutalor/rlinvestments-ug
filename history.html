History

Our history

RL Investments Ltd has moved goods and fuel on Ugandan roads since 2012.

Milestones

Key moments

  1. Founding

    RL Investments Ltd is established

    Rodger Lutalo founds RL Investments Ltd as a privately owned Ugandan company, with its head office in Kampala.

  2. 2012

    Operations begin

    The company starts operations in secondary petroleum transport, delivering fuel from depots to retail service stations for oil marketing companies.

  3. Fleet investment

    New tankers bought direct from the manufacturer

    RL Investments buys new Sinotruk HOWO fuel tankers directly from the manufacturer and brings them into service in Uganda.

  4. Recognition

    Best Secondary Transporter

    TotalEnergies names RL Investments Best Secondary Transporter for outstanding performance in HSEQ and Transportation Operations Services.

  5. Today

    A diversified transport and logistics business

    RL Investments runs dry cargo and petroleum transport for manufacturers, distributors, FMCG companies, industrial enterprises, commercial organisations and oil marketing companies, with box trucks, flatbeds and fuel tankers.
