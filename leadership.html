Leadership

Leadership and governance

RL Investments Ltd is a privately owned limited company, registered in Uganda, company registration number 80010003131364. Clear accountability runs from management to every driver on the road.

Founder (Managing Director 2012–2024)

Rodger Lutalo

Rodger Lutalo founded RL Investments Ltd and, as Managing Director (2012–2024), built the company around safe, dependable transport and distribution.

Under his leadership the company grew from secondary petroleum transport into a diversified logistics business serving manufacturers, distributors, FMCG companies, industrial enterprises and oil marketing companies, and introduced the custom ERP system and GPS fleet tracking that its operations run on today.

Governance

How we govern the business

Company management sets direction, approves policies and reviews operational, financial and safety performance. Managers are accountable for their areas and report on them regularly.

Governance principles

  • Honest, transparent dealings with customers and partners
  • Clear roles and accountability
  • Project management (PMO) frameworks and KPI standards for performance and change
  • Compliance with Ugandan law and customer requirements
  • Safety performance reviewed alongside commercial results
  • Accurate records of trips, deliveries and costs
