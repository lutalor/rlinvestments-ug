Privacy Policy

How RL Investments Ltd handles information collected through this website.

Effective date: 13 November 2026

RL Investments Limited ("RL Investments", "we", "us") is a transport, logistics and distribution company registered in Uganda (No. 80010003131364), of Plot 3, 2nd Street, Industrial Area, Kampala, Uganda (P.O. Box 21739, Kampala). We are the data controller for personal information collected through this website, rlinvestments.ug.

Information we collect

Enquiries you send us. When you use the enquiry form on our Contact page, or contact us through the WhatsApp button, we receive the details you provide: your name, company, contact details, and the information about your transport requirement that you include, such as cargo, collection and delivery points, volumes and timing.

Technical information. Our website host and form provider automatically process technical data when you visit the site or submit the form, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in their server logs. This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies and does not run analytics.

How we use your information

We use enquiry information only to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations, provide our transport and logistics services, keep our business records, and meet our legal obligations. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use enquiry details for unrelated marketing.

Who processes information for us

We use a small number of service providers to run this website:

  • GitHub, Inc. hosts this website (GitHub Pages).
  • Formspree, Inc. processes enquiry form submissions and delivers them to us.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries any conversation you start through the WhatsApp button, under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
  • Google Maps opens only if you click the directions link on our Contact page; Google's own privacy policy then applies.

Some of these providers are located outside Uganda and may process information in other countries. We share enquiry details with other parties only where necessary to handle your enquiry or where the law requires it.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry information for as long as it is needed to deal with the enquiry, maintain the business relationship and meet record-keeping obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, you may ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you may object to how we use it. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address above or on Tel / WhatsApp +256 777 188 766. You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Office, Uganda.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
