Our systems

Technology that keeps every truck in view

Dispatch works from the same custom ERP: live GPS positions feed journey planning, so dispatchers, managers and customers can see where each truck is as it happens and act early when a delivery is at risk.

ERP

One system for the business

Our custom-built ERP is the single system our teams use to plan and record operations, so everyone works from the same data.

GPS

GPS fleet tracking

Trucks in our own fleet carry GPS units integrated with the ERP. They show each truck’s location and journey progress in real time, so delays are spotted and acted on early.

Dispatch

Data-driven dispatch

Dispatchers assign trucks and plan routes using live fleet data, with SLA alerts that flag deliveries at risk of running late.

Reporting

KPI dashboards

Dashboards track our key performance indicators against agreed standards, giving managers and customers accurate, timely reporting.

Customers

Real-time delivery visibility

Customers get real-time visibility of their deliveries, so they can plan around actual arrival times instead of chasing updates.

People

Trained to use the data

Operations staff and dispatchers are trained to use the GPS and dashboards every day, and we measure adoption by how the tools are actually used.

Performance

How we measure service

Our ERP records every trip by truck, customer and load, and GPS data shows when each delivery actually arrived. Together they let us review performance with customers and improve where we fall short.
