Transport enquiries

For quotations or to discuss a transport contract, call our office or send an enquiry using the form below, and tell us:

  • What you need moved: dry cargo or fuel (PMS, AGO)
  • Collection point and delivery locations
  • Expected volumes and delivery frequency
  • Your preferred start date

A note on fraud

RL Investments Ltd never charges a fee for job applications or recruitment, and never asks customers, suppliers or job seekers to send payments to a personal mobile money number. If someone contacts you claiming to represent RL Investments and asking for money, do not pay. Call our office on the number shown on this page to check before you act.
