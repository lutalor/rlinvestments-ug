Our story

From petroleum transport to a diversified logistics business

RL Investments Ltd is a privately owned transport, logistics and distribution company founded by Rodger Lutalo. The company began operations in 2012 and first built its reputation in the petroleum sector, carrying fuel for oil marketing companies.

Through steady investment in trucks, people and safety systems, the company has grown into a diversified logistics business. Today we serve manufacturers, distributors, FMCG companies, industrial enterprises, commercial organisations and oil marketing companies, with box trucks, flatbeds and fuel tankers.

We run the business on a custom ERP system. Through the ERP project, every truck in our company-owned fleet was fitted with a GPS unit for real-time monitoring. It has improved planning, reduced complaints about delivery delays, and gives customers real-time visibility of their deliveries.

We invest in our people, our trucks and our safety systems because our customers depend on deliveries arriving on time and in full.

How a delivery works

From depot to station: the life of one delivery

Every load, dry cargo or fuel, follows the same disciplined path, so customers know what happens at each stage and can follow their delivery as it moves.

  1. 1

    Enquiry and quotation

    You tell us what needs to move, the collection point, delivery locations, expected volumes and frequency, and your preferred start date. We confirm trucks, route and rate before any load moves.

  2. 2

    Planning the journey

    The route is planned on our ERP system, with a route risk assessment and a journey plan for the driver. A trained, assessed driver and a maintained, inspected truck are assigned to the load.

  3. 3

    Loading

    Dry cargo is loaded and secured for the road ahead. Fuel is loaded at the Jinja or Kampala depots against the customer’s delivery documents, into compartmented tankers fitted with hazard placards and safety equipment.

  4. 4

    On the road

    Dispatch follows the journey by GPS as it happens. Customers can see where their delivery is, and delays are spotted and acted on early rather than explained afterwards.

  5. 5

    Delivery and documentation

    On a multi-drop route, each drop is recorded against its own delivery invoice, so volumes delivered reconcile with volumes loaded. Delivery documentation is completed for every load.

  6. 6

    Review and reporting

    The trip is recorded in the ERP by truck, customer and load. Dashboards report performance against agreed standards, and we review the results with the customer.
